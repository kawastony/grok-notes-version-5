**Date:** 2026-10-11

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism. One stuck problem, not the rotation curve.

## Question

Can a lattice of lapse-bearing cells produce a cumulative delay, defocusing, or angular effect at galactic scale?

## Subquestions, kept separate

A. Observable: travel-time delay or defocusing accumulated over cells. Not rotation speed.

B. Sector: Box B (lapse). Topology (Box A) enters only if the path winds and the holonomy is asked for. Closure (Box C) is bookkeeping. Anchor (Box D) sets the cell size.

C. Missing input: cell length (the anchor), number of cells, and the lapse scale ξ per cell. None is derived from the static cone.

## Branches to compare, when run

- ν = 0
- ν = 1/4
- ν = 1/2

Classify the result as robust, branch-dependent, or anchor-dependent. The midpoint identity can track a quadratic sample of the delay across a cell. The integral ledger can sum the delay. Neither produces the cell size.

## Not in this plan
