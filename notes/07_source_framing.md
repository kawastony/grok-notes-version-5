## Source A — criterion, not object

Short answer: do not translate the object. Translate the criterion. "Clock," "scale," and "coupling" are homonyms across the two works, and joining them by vocabulary is the mapping-move the audit already caught. What connects is the standard used to judge both works. That standard has three grades, not two. Micro sits a grade above macro. That ranking is the translation: it links the two through a shared yardstick without pretending their objects meet.

1. Grade the gains. Derived versus mapped was a binary. It resolves into three grades. Grade 1: form derived, constant derived. Grade 2: form derived, constant fitted. Grade 3: form chosen, constant fitted. Micro lives in 1 and 2. Macro lives in 3. The translating sentence: the standard that places the galactic ruler in grade 3 places the packet's split in grades 1 and 2. Grades 1 and 2 cannot rescue grade 3.

2. Two-column register. Length: internal yes, physical conversion no. Time: internal yes, physical conversion no. Coupling: internal split yes, field coupling no. Universality: no.

3. Split every overloaded noun. Rabi period is not a physical time standard. Decay length is not an acceleration scale.

4. Turn "no bridge" into an interface spec. Sockets: a0 dimensionful, the mu-form, a0(z). Each unconnected.

5. Open with a scope firewall. This note translates micro gains into shared vocabulary. It asserts no bridge. A sentence that reads as one is a homonym or a spec, not a result.

6. Name the three imports. Lattice spacing to physical length. An energy unit to hbar. A grad Phi or g term.

Analogy: two tax returns on the same form. Same form is not the same business.

One line from the source: build the translation on the shared criterion, not the shared noun. The audit that debits the macro ruler is the same audit that credits the micro packet. That symmetry is the only bridge the evidence supports. Read "bridge" in that sentence as the audit, not a mechanism. Note 3 keeps the second reading out.

Strain recorded in the source, and answered in note 6: the v = 1 fit is grade 2 only if exp(-2 v d) and A(v) are forced. If the exponential were chosen, micro would drop to grade 3. "Derived" must be operational. A reader can hear "same standard" as nearness. The tax-form line is the guard.
