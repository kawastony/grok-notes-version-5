# Note 30 — Changing metric, time and space

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Proper time and circumference change. Packet not detuned.

## What changes

Take ds^2 = -dt^2 + dr^2 + (alpha(t) r)^2 d phi^2 during the active interval, and alpha constant during pause.

Space: the proper circumference at fixed r is 2 pi alpha(t) r. It changes only while alpha changes. In the slice of note 29 it shrinks by a quarter. Distances along a generator, the r direction, do not change in this metric. A different elongation, growing slant at fixed base radius, changes the range of r instead. Either way the change is confined to the active interval.

Time: a static observer has d tau = dt, because g_tt = -1. Coordinate time and proper time match for that observer. A moving observer has d tau^2 = dt^2 - dr^2 - alpha(t)^2 r^2 d phi^2, so the spatial term in the proper-time integral changes while the funnel elongates. Clocks that travel around the cone disagree before and after the active interval. Clocks that sit still do not.

The geodesic equation itself changes only in the active interval. Pause restores note 22.

## What does not change

The change in alpha is even: it does not pick a direction along a wall coordinate. Note 11 still gives a common shift, not sigma_z. The kink mass v is not alpha. Note 19 still needs an identification that is not forced.

A changing deficit is a changing source. It is not g, and it is not a0. Einstein's equation in the interior remains the flat one off the tip. The elongation does not become a derived ruler.

Step 1 stays closed. The new statement is only that active-phase geodesics are geodesics of a time-dependent metric, and pause-phase geodesics are the static ones.
