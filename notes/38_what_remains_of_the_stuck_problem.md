# Note 38 — What remains of the version-4 stuck problem

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Separation. Optical lattice advanced. Packet bridge not.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism.

Version 4 was stuck on two different problems that had been run together.

1. The optical / lattice problem: can a lattice of lapse-bearing cells produce a cumulative delay or defocus at large N? Note 36 scoped it. Note 37 ran the self-similar case. Delay is branch-blind by the transverse-independence of the lapse. Defocus is branch-dependent with rate ν ln ρ. That problem has a result.

2. The packet-to-galaxy bridge: can the kink packet (zero mode, ΔE = A(v) exp(-2 v d), transfer clock) supply a universal acceleration, a unit map, or a rotation curve? Notes 10, 13, 31, and 35 closed it for the present field content. The packet has no g. The static cone has no time and no preferred length. The lattice result uses a hosted ξ and a hosted ρ. It does not inherit ΔE or the transfer clock.

Progress can be made on the optical side: vary ξ_i across cells, vary cell ratios, bound the N at which a given ν becomes unreasonable, and decide whether delay or defocus is the primary observable for a chosen galaxy measurement. That work does not reopen the packet bridge.

The remaining hard problem is the anchor. One more measurement or constraint is required before (v, d) can be fit. Image separation can pin μ. Nothing in note 37 supplies the length or the speed.
