# Note 2 — Translation into a two-level microscopic theory

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Dictionary of the locked packet. Not a new derivation. Not a galactic or cosmic theory.

## Why this theory and not another

The packet already is a one-dimensional Dirac domain wall,

H = -i sigma_y d/dx + m(x) sigma_z,

with m changing sign at each wall. Its near-zero subspace is two-dimensional. Projecting onto that subspace is a two-level Schrödinger problem. That problem is the microscopic theory of an SSH soliton pair and of a Kitaev end-mode pair. Every locked pillar is a statement in that theory. Nothing outside the near-zero subspace is required.

A galactic reading needs a coupling g and a clock that is the same for every system. Neither is in H. That reading is not recovered by the translation.

## Dictionary

| Packet | Two-level theory |
|--------|------------------|
| Sign-changing mass wall | Domain wall in the SSH dimerization, or in the Kitaev pairing | 
| Exact zero on one wall | Midgap state, or one Majorana end mode |
| Split Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d) | Hybridization of the left and right modes |
| Decay length 1/(2v) | Localization length of each end mode |
| Transfer at T = pi / Delta E | Half-cycle of the two-level oscillation |
| Middle weight fixed | Even/odd combinations share the same middle density, so the cross term cancels there |
| Norm stays 1 | Probability conservation inside the projected subspace |
| Chirality unresolved | The numerical kernel is a mixture; the continuum chirality is not a result of the run |

## What carries

In the left/right wall basis the projected Hamiltonian is

H_2 = (Delta E / 2) sigma_x,

with

Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d),

A(v) = 4^v Gamma(v + 1/2) / (sqrt(pi) Gamma(v)),

and xi = 1/(2v). Eigenvalues are +Delta E/2 and -Delta E/2. A state seeded on the left wall is the equal superposition. It arrives on the right wall at

T = pi / Delta E,

and returns at 2T. That is the transfer clock. It is a Rabi half-period of this two-level system, in lattice units.

The locked numbers sit inside this reading. At v = 1 the fit is Delta E = 1.772 exp(-d/0.509), against xi = 1/2. At v = 2 the fitted length is 0.255, against 1/4. At d = 2, v = 1, the split is 0.07084. Left weight runs 0.871, 0.437, 0.003 at t/T = 0, 0.5, 1. Right weight runs the reverse. Middle stays 0.126. Norm stays 1. The fixed middle is what the two-level theory requires: the bonding and antibonding densities agree in the overlap, so interference does not move that weight.

Recurrence and the two limits carry as properties of A(v), not as new inputs. A(v+1) = 4 (v+1/2)/v A(v). A ~ v as v -> 0. A ~ 4^v sqrt(v/pi) as v grows.

## What does not carry

- No g. The split is an overlap, not a response to an external field. The free-energy response stays zero.
- No unit map. E* = hbar c / a and v = m0 c^2 / E* remain a dictionary. a and m0 are not fixed by A(v).
- No universal acceleration. d (Delta E)^2 changes with v and d.
- No 18.7 eV, no 1.41e-10 or 8.25e-11 m/s^2, no 12.1 kpc, no z_t.
- No Callias count on the tested hedgehog boxes. The one-dimensional ancestor is not the three-dimensional theorem.
- No interpolator from this Hamiltonian.
- Black-hole surface gravity is set by the hole's mass. It is not Delta E.
- Chirality of the numerical kernel stays an unresolved mixture. Opposite chirality of the antikink is the continuum statement, not a result of the run.

## Claim level

The translation is an isomorphism of the near-zero subspace onto a two-level theory already used for SSH soliton pairs and Kitaev end modes. It does not identify a or m0. It does not add a term. A later theory that claims the galactic ruler must add a coupling and a shared clock. That term is not a recovery of this packet.
