# Note 17 — Energy across one active and two pauses

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Reading tested. Step 1 stays closed.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism.

## Proposal

Energy moves from one bridge to the next. The balance runs across one active bridge and two pause bridges, the v4 triple, not a new object.

Version 4 already has a piece of this. Note 34: pause is parked membership, active is the jump, feed belongs to the transit. Early Delta W8 is +0.088 on A, about +0.03 to +0.05 on P1, and about 0 on P2. Conservation across a pause keeps v and I, and keeps the index. No packet was measured leaving A and arriving at the edge.

## What the proposal can change

It can rename the packet. The two pauses are the parked walls. The active bridge is the transit. The earned transfer, left weight 0.871 to 0.003 and right weight the reverse at t/T = 0, 0.5, 1, with middle weight 0.126 and norm 1, is then the balance: energy leaves one pause, crosses the active bridge, and arrives at the other. The fixed middle is the part that does not move. That identification is a reading. It was not measured on the operator tubes.

## What it does not change

A sum E_A + E_P1 + E_P2 = constant is internal bookkeeping. It does not add g. The mass profile of the two parked walls stays even, so note 15 still applies: the packet cannot tilt itself. An even balance projects to I. It does not project to sigma_z.

The balance does not freeze Delta E / d. The transit clock is T = pi / Delta E, and Delta E still runs as A(v) exp(-2 v d). At v = 1 the critical acceleration of an imported tilt is still 164 times larger at separation 1 than at separation 3. Nothing in the three-bridge sum selects one vd.

It does not supply a length or an energy unit. The cone sector remains the other enlargement, and note 16 still stands.

## Reopen condition

The balance would matter for the bridge only if the ledger itself fixed an odd scalar, or fixed Delta E / d, from the same data that fix m(x). A conserved sum across A, P1, and P2 does not. Step 1 stays closed. Steps 2 and 3 stay unopened.
