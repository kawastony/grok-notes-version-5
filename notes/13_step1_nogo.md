# Note 13 — Step 1 no-go

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Fork closed for the present Hamiltonian. Steps 2 and 3 not opened.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism.

## No-go

Let the isolated class be Hamiltonians of the form H = -i sigma_y d/dx + m(x) sigma_z with m real and even, parameterized by the wall data (v, d). No coordinate of this class is an external acceleration or an external gradient.

Therefore partial F / partial g = 0 on the class. Any sigma_z term in the two-mode block requires an operator outside the class. Note 11: an even addition gives only I. Note 12: an odd scalar V = g x gives kappa = -1 + O(exp(-2 v d)), and that scalar is the outside operator.

The present Hamiltonian acquires a detuning only by import. Step 1 fails for this class.

## Consequence

Steps 2 and 3 are not live. A modulus condition on v d, and a unit map, would still be grading an imported tilt. They are not attacked here.

The conditional object of note 9 stands as conditional: standard mu is the imbalance after the import, and g_c = Delta E / (2 d) is not universal. Kappa = -1 does not freeze g_c.

## What would reopen the fork

A reduction whose field content already includes an odd scalar or a metric factor, with that factor fixed by the same theory that fixes m(x), and with kappa computed rather than written in. The kink packet as transferred does not have that field content. Adding it is a different theory.

## Standing result

Packet remains grades 1 and 2 on its own. Galactic ruler remains grade 3. Full bridge is not shown. The Step 1 fork is closed by this no-go unless the field content changes.
