# Note 29 — The funnel is the metric, not the geodesic

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Category split. Einstein geodesics on a frozen cone are note 22.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism. The cone was used in both the macro and the micro notes. That shared use is not a shared object.

## Two different things

The funnel is a surface, or a metric. A geodesic is a curve on that metric satisfying the equation of note 24. Identifying the elongating funnel with a geodesic joins the geometry to a path on it. The proposal is kept as a different statement: the geodesics change because the metric changes.

Einstein's free-fall equation is the geodesic equation of the spacetime metric. On the interior of the static cone, off the tip, the Riemann tensor is zero, note 26. The geodesics are the straight lines on the unrolled sector, note 22. There is no extra Einstein term in the interior.

## Pause and active

Pause: alpha and the slant range are constant. The geodesic equation is note 22. Curves do not change their law.

Active: the funnel elongates, so alpha = alpha(t), or the range of r grows, note 20. phi is still cyclic, so l = alpha(t)^2 r^2 d phi/d lambda remains conserved. The radial equation gains alpha-dot terms, because the metric depends on time. An instantaneous straight line on the unrolling is not the true curve while alpha is changing.

A slice: alpha from 0.8 in pause to 0.6 at the end of an active interval, at r = 2. Circumference 2 pi alpha r goes from 10.05 to 7.54. The deficit 1 - alpha goes from 0.2 to 0.4. A vacuum cone has constant deficit. Changing it needs a source. That source is not in the kink Hamiltonian.
