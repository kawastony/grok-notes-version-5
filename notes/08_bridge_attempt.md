# Note 8 — Full-bridge attempt

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Attempt opened. Bridge not shown.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism. The aim of this note is the full bridge: a derived packet-to-ruler coupling, a universal scale, and a unit map. Notes 3 to 6 stay in force. A candidate that needs an import is marked as an import.

Four revival paths were named. Each is tested below or in notes 9 and 10.

1. A g-dependent term forced by the packet reduction.
2. A constraint that fixes v and d for every packet.
3. A unit map identified rather than fitted.
4. A smaller macro interface, so less has to be derived.

The bare packet still has partial F / partial g = 0. Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d) does not contain g. Path 1 is closed for the isolated Hamiltonian. Note 9 reopens it only by importing the Newtonian potential, and records what that import does and does not yield.

No stationary condition in the packet fixes (v, d). Path 2 is closed on the present Hamiltonian.

Path 3 remains the dictionary E* = hbar c / a, v = m0 c^2 / E*. Nothing in the tail product sets a or m0. The 18.7 eV mediator is not this split. Tying the scale to H0 was already a worse galaxy fit, factor 1.30, in the version-4 record. Not rerun.

Path 4 does not shrink the interface. Deep MOND still needs a dimensionful a0 and a join. The cone does not force either. Note 9 shows that one join appears only after the field is imported, and that the scale of that join is not universal.
