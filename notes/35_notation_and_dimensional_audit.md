## Dimensional no-go

The static cone metric has no time and no preferred length. λ is dimensionless. Therefore it produces no observable with dimensions of speed or length.

- A speed v requires a fundamental speed. The cone does not supply one.
- A length d requires a length. The cone does not supply one.

With imports {c_0, R_0, μ} against data {v, d} the count is three parameters, two data, one short. The natural extra datum already in hand is the image separation, which measures μ and nothing in the local sector supplies. The anchor remains external.
