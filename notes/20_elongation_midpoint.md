## Geometry

Base radius R fixed. The funnel elongates by a slant length ell. Depth and half-angle are then not free,

h = sqrt(ell^2 - R^2), sin theta = R / ell.

Lengthening at fixed R shrinks the opening. A numerical slice, R = 1, ell = 1.2, 1.525, 2.0, gives h = 0.663, 1.151, 1.732 and theta = 56.4, 41.0, 30.0 degrees. The half-angle drops by 26 degrees over that lengthening.

## Geodesics are not the fall

On the unrolled sector a geodesic is a straight line. It carries no preferred direction down the generator. A fall needs a gradient along the slant. Calling that gradient gravity is the analogy. It is not the geodesic equation, and it is not a term in the kink Hamiltonian.

The tool tracks the elongation. It does not derive the potential the picture is not claimed to be.
