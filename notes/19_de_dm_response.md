## Packet side

The split is the tail product,

Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d),

A(v) = 4^v Gamma(v+1/2) / (sqrt(pi) Gamma(v)).

If a seep changes the wall mass v at fixed separation,

d Delta E / Delta E = ( ln 4 + psi(v+1/2) - psi(v) - 2 d ) dv.

psi is the digamma function. At v = 1 the digamma piece equals 2, so

d Delta E = Delta E (2 - 2 d) dv.

At d = 1 this derivative vanishes. At d = 2 it is -2 Delta E dv. At d = 3 it is -4 Delta E dv. The zero at (v, d) = (1, 1) is a stationary point of the split against a mass change. It is not a selected packet: d is still free, and the imported g_c still runs with d.
