# Note 4 — Lattice to physical ledger

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Register. Columns do not meet.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism. Each micro quantity answers three questions: what is derived internally, what a physical reading would need, and whether that need is supplied.

| quantity | derived on the lattice | required to be physical | supplied? |
|----------|------------------------|-------------------------|-----------|
| length | decay length 1/(2v) | lattice spacing to a metre | no |
| time | Rabi period T = pi / Delta E | hbar, or an energy unit | no |
| coupling | Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d) | a term in g or grad Phi | no |
| universality | none | a constraint fixing v and d for every packet | no |

The right column is what the macro side reads. The left column is what the packet supplies. The register connects the two readings by stating the missing conversion. It does not assert that the columns meet.

## Split nouns

- Clock. Micro: Rabi period, internal, per packet, lattice units. Macro: physical time standard, external, shared. Not one word for both.
- Ruler. Decay length is not an acceleration scale.
- Coupling. Overlap split is not a field coupling.

Overloaded nouns are where a bridge enters unannounced. The table is the block.

## Three imports

A later theory that claims the galactic ruler must add exactly three things. They are not a recovery of this packet.

1. Lattice spacing to a physical length.
2. An energy unit to hbar.
3. A grad Phi term, or a coupling g.

Dictionary only, still unfixed: E* = hbar c / a, v = m0 c^2 / E*, xi_phys = hbar / (2 m0 c), Delta E_phys = E* Delta E. a and m0 are conversions. They are not parameters of A(v).

## Refusals

No 18.7 eV. No 1.41e-10 or 8.25e-11 m/s^2. No 12.1 kpc. No z_t. No g in the split, so the free-energy response stays zero. d (Delta E)^2 changes with v and d, so it is not a universal acceleration.
