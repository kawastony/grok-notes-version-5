# Note 10 — Dimensional stop

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Full bridge blocked. Next forced step named.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism.

The lattice theory has one scale, v. Energies scale as v. Lengths scale as 1/v. Any acceleration built from the packet, including g_c = Delta E / (2 d), scales as v^2 times a function of the dimensionless product v d. A change of lattice unit multiplies that acceleration by the unit factor. An SI acceleration is not among the outputs.

To reach m/s^2 the missing imports are still the three in note 4: a length, hbar, and a field coupling. Note 9 used the third and did not generate the first two. Removing the lattice unit without putting a physical length in is not a step the Hamiltonian can take.

## What would count as the bridge

All three, forced, not fitted.

1. A detuning linear in the local field, produced by the packet reduction rather than written in as Phi.
2. A condition that fixes v d, and fixes Delta E / d, to one value for every packet.
3. An identification of the lattice unit with a length and an energy that are not the galactic a0 being derived.

None of the three is in the present record. A fit of g_c to 1.2e-10 m/s^2 would be grade 3. It is not done here.

## Standing result

The packet remains grades 1 and 2 on its own: zero mode, tail-product split, transfer clock, fixed middle. The standard interpolator is the imbalance of that packet only after a Newtonian tilt is imported, and the critical acceleration of that tilt is not universal. The galactic ruler remains grade 3. The full bridge is not shown.
