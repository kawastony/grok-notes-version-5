# Note 23 — Geodesics do not reopen the bridge

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Exploration closed for the packet. Equations kept.

A shared audit scale is not a shared physical mechanism.

The cone geodesic is a free straight line on a sector. Its only scale is the impact parameter b, or the deficit alpha. Neither is fixed by the kink data (v, d). Neither is an odd scalar along the wall coordinate.

Placing the packet on a generator identifies the kink coordinate with r. Note 18 already marked that identification as unforced. Even after it, a geodesic that misses the tip is even in the unrolled transverse coordinate. It does not prefer one wall. A geodesic that hits the tip is removed from the surface in finite time. It is not a transfer clock.

An elongating funnel, fixed base radius, changing slant, is not this metric. The geodesic equation would have to be rewritten on a time-dependent alpha or a time-dependent range of r. That is a new system. It is not solved here, and note 21 already found that the lengthening does not force D proportional to enclosed mass.

Step 1 stays closed. The equations are the intrinsic geometry of the cone. They are not the packet, and they are not the ruler.
